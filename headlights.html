The specialty

Cloudy headlights aren't just ugly — they're a visibility problem

Houston sun oxidizes the polycarbonate coating on most headlights within 3–5 years, cutting nighttime light output by up to 80%. We restore it, curbside, in under an hour.

Why not just buy a $12 kit?

Store kits skip the UV-blocking topcoat. Lenses look clear for a few weeks, then yellow faster than before because the sanded surface is now unprotected. We seal every restoration with a catalyzed clear coat rated for outdoor UV exposure.

The process

  1. Inspect & Tape Off

    We check for cracks or moisture inside the housing, then mask the surrounding paint and trim.

  2. Wet-Sand in Stages

    400 to 3000-grit, working the oxidized layer off without going near the paint edge.

  3. Machine Polish

    A compound buff brings back full optical clarity — not just a surface shine.

  4. UV Seal & Cure

    A catalyzed clear coat goes on last, curing on-site so you're driving with it same day.
