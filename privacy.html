Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026. This policy covers our website and our mobile apps.

The short version

We collect only what we need to book and perform your service — your name, phone number, ZIP code, and the details of the job. We never sell your information, we don't run ads, and there are no third-party trackers or analytics SDKs in our apps. Your booking details are visible only to you and to ClearBeam AutoCare.

What we collect

When you book (website or customer app): your name, phone number, ZIP code, the service you chose, your preferred date and time, and anything you write about your vehicle. That's it — no account required.

In the owner app (used only by ClearBeam AutoCare): the owner's login email and an encrypted session token stored securely on the device, plus a push notification token so the owner is alerted the moment a new booking comes in.

How we use it

Your information is used for one purpose: scheduling, confirming, and completing your service. We may call or text you about your appointment. We do not use your information for marketing lists, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone except as required by law.

Where it's stored

Booking data is stored on our secure backend hosting (United States). Access is limited to the business owner. Session tokens in the owner app expire automatically after 30 days.

Cookies & tracking

Our website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. Our mobile apps contain no advertising SDKs and no cross-app tracking.

Your choices

Want to see, correct, or delete your booking information? Email khaled@clearbeamandautocare.com or call/text (206) 403-3356 and we'll take care of it.

Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the website or apps after a change means you accept the updated policy.
